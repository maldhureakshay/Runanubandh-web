We respect your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to permanently delete your Mali Runanubandh account and all associated data directly from within the app.

1 How to Delete Your Account

Account deletion is available directly inside the Mali Runanubandh app. Follow these steps:

1

Open the App

Log in to your Mali Runanubandh account

2

Go to Your Profile

Tap on Your Profile from the main screen

3

Select Delete Profile

Tap "Delete Profile" from the profile options

4

Confirm Deletion

Confirm your choice — your account will be permanently deleted

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Once confirmed, your account and all associated data will be scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days.

2 What Happens to Your Data

Upon account deletion, we permanently erase all of your personal data. There is no partial retention — we do a full deletion.

Data Type Action Timeframe
Profile information (name, age, photos, bio) ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Contact details (phone, email) ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Community / caste details ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Messages and interactions ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Device identifiers & Firebase tokens ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Login history & app usage data ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Biodata PDF files ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Uploaded images & profile photos ✓ Permanently Deleted Within 30 days

ℹ️ Note: Data that other users have already viewed or saved (such as screenshots taken by them) cannot be retrieved or erased, as it is outside our control. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for more details.

3 Deletion Timeline

After you confirm deletion in the app:

  • Your account is immediately deactivated — your profile will no longer be visible to other users
  • All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • You will receive a confirmation notification once deletion is complete
  • You cannot log back in after initiating deletion
